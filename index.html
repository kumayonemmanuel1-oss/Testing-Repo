CEO& Founder
The Visionary Behind It All

Olaniyi
Eja Nla

— The Big Fish —

Born and raised in the historic city of Abeokuta, Ogun State, Olaniyi — widely known as Eja Nla (The Big Fish) — is the founder and chief executive behind 2Up 2Down Apartments. His journey is one of vision, relentless drive, and a deep commitment to elevating the short-let hospitality experience in Southwest Nigeria.

With a keen eye for detail and an uncompromising standard for excellence, Olaniyi built 2Up 2Down from the ground up — transforming premium spaces in Ota into destinations where guests don't just stay, they experience. Every design choice, every amenity, and every policy reflects his personal philosophy: comfort without compromise.

As a proud son of Ogun State, he remains deeply invested in the growth of the local hospitality economy — creating a luxury alternative that competes with the best, right here at home.

"I didn't build this for the few — I built it for everyone who deserves to feel like royalty, even if just for a night."
